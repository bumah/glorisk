Risk and return — together.

Most investment tools focus on returns. GloRisk shows you what they're missing — risk. Returns tell you how much an asset gained or lost. They don't tell you whether it matches your risk appetite in the short or long term. GloRisk does. A risk scorecard built for you. Without it, you're only seeing half the information you need.

How it works

Three steps to your personal scorecard

1

Build your scorecard

Answer 20 questions about the sectors, markets, style and risk you care about. This is your scorecard — your thresholds, your rules, not a default template.

2

We flag the data

We track 36 risk indicators on every asset across 6 categories and flag each one green, amber or red against published thresholds. No opinions, no buy/sell calls — just the data.

3

Rank by match

Every asset is scored against your scorecard and ranked by match %. The judgement stays with you.
