Your Global Risk Companion

Most investors rely on hype, hope, and instinct — without a clear understanding of risk. They enter positions without knowing how unstable an asset is, how much it can drop, or whether it matches how they invest. This leads to misaligned decisions, avoidable losses, and poor outcomes.

What is GloRisk?

GloRisk is a risk intelligence layer for your investments. It gives you:

• A clear view of risk across global assets
• A way to compare opportunities objectively
• Insight into your potential downside
• A personalised score based on how you invest

So you invest with discipline, clarity, and control.

How the GloRisk Score works

You complete a 10-question profile about your investment preferences — how you feel about volatility, drawdowns, trends, returns, and growth.

Each question maps to one of our 10 risk indicators. Your answer determines how strictly you score each indicator:

Strict — only green indicators score well. Amber and red are penalised.
Moderate — green and amber score well. Red is penalised.
Open — all indicator states score well. You're comfortable with risk.

The result is a score from 0 to 100 that tells you how well each asset fits your risk profile — not whether it's objectively good or bad.

A conservative investor and an aggressive investor will see completely different scores for the same stock. That's the point.
