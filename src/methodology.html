About GloRisk

Investment Rankings. For You.
Not the Crowd.

Risk is personal. Most investment tools forgot that.

Every screener, every top pick list, every analyst recommendation is built on the same flawed assumption — that risk means the same thing to everyone.

It doesn't. Your risk appetite depends on your goals, your timeline, your tolerance for loss. Yet the tools available to most investors ignore this entirely — ranking assets against a generic benchmark that represents nobody in particular.

The financial industry has spent decades perfecting recommendations for the average investor. The problem? The average investor doesn't exist.

GloRisk is built differently. We score every stock, crypto, and ETF globally against your personal risk profile — so the ranking you see is built for you. Not the crowd. Not the analyst. Not an algorithm trained on someone else's preferences.
