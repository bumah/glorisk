What we do

Most screeners, top picks and analyst recommendations are opinions dressed up as scores. A single rating smuggles in someone else's view of risk, growth and horizon.

GloRisk takes a different path. We don't rate assets. We flag data against published thresholds.

For every asset, we track 36 risk indicators across 6 categories — market risk, volatility, trend, fundamentals, liquidity and sentiment — and colour each one green, amber or red against thresholds that are in the open. You then build your own scorecard: what you accept, what you tolerate, what you reject. We score every asset against your scorecard and rank by match.

No buy calls. No sell calls. No weighted composite score that hides its ingredients. Just the data, the thresholds, and your scorecard — three layers you can inspect separately.

Data is universal. Scorecards are personal.

Every investor sees the same 36 indicators and the same RAG thresholds. What changes is the scorecard you bring — because only you know what you're willing to hold.
